**Build vs buy AI** is the decision every growing company hits: subscribe to AI SaaS, or build a custom tool around your workflows?

There is no universal winner. There is a clear decision framework.

## When AI SaaS is the right buy

Choose a vendor when:

- The problem is common (support chat, meeting notes, generic writing)  
- Speed matters more than deep integration  
- You can live with their data residency and security model  
- Switching cost is acceptable if they raise prices  

Buying AI SaaS gets you features fast. You pay with **subscription fees, less control, and roadmap risk**.

## When a custom AI tool wins

Build (or commission) custom when:

- Your workflow is the product. Generics create more clicks, not less  
- Data cannot leave your stack or region  
- You need agents tied to **your** CRM, ERP, or internal APIs  
- Differentiation depends on proprietary process knowledge  

Custom tools cost more up front. They win when software must **grow with your business**, not bend your process to a template.

## A practical decision matrix

Ask:

1. Is this workflow unique enough to justify ownership?  
2. Will we still need this in 24 months?  
3. Can a SaaS API plug into our systems with acceptable security?  
4. Do we have (or can we hire) people to maintain a custom system?  
5. What is the cost of being wrong lost deals, compliance, or thrash?  

If three or more answers push toward ownership, lean custom. Otherwise buy and integrate.

## Hybrid is often best

Many teams **buy the model layer** (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) and **build the product layer** auth, permissions, RAG over private docs, NestJS APIs, audit logs. You rent intelligence; you own the workflow.

## Avoid these traps

- Building because engineering is bored  
- Buying five overlapping AI SaaS tools with no owner  
- Custom builds with no evaluation or success metric  
- Ignoring exit costs from either path  

## Recommendation

Start with buy for commodity capabilities. Build custom AI when the workflow, data, or differentiation is yours. Revisit the choice every major release, not once a year in a panic.
